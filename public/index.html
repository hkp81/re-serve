🌿 About Our Mission

Re-serve: Serving Again for a Better Planet

Every day, millions of delicious, safe, and untouched meals from institutional messes, corporate buffets, and bakeries are discarded simply because of unpredictable footfall. Re-serve builds the civic bridge that redistributes food before it spoils—transforming waste into hope.

🍱

1. Economic Sustainability

Partners recover food preparation costs by selling surplus at 50%–75% discounts. The platform charges a modest 5%–10% commission, ensuring long-term technological and logistical self-reliance.

🤝

2. Zero Hunger (UN SDG 2)

Excess food batches are listed as ₹0 humanitarian donations, instantly claimable by verified NGOs, charitable shelters, and student volunteers. ₹0 platform fee is ever charged for humanitarian rescue.

🛡️

3. Dignity-First Handover

We reject the stigma of charitable soup lines. Every transaction generates a standard Dynamic QR Code and 6-Digit OTP. The collection experience is confidential, respectful, and contactless.

⚠️ Food Safety Advisory, FSSAI Standards & Good Samaritan Notice

Re-serve operates under the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Surplus Food Regulations and international Good Samaritan food donation frameworks. All food partners must adhere to stringent thermal holding regimes before listing.

🔥 Hot-Holding Mandate: Cooked items must be maintained continuously at ≥ 63°C in steam chafer tables or insulated containers.
❄️ Cold-Chain Mandate: Dairy, salads, and bakery patisserie must be held at ≤ 4°C in verified commercial refrigerators.
⏳ Strict 2-Hour / 4-Hour Rule: Items entering the temperature danger zone (5°C–60°C) are decommissioned after 4 hours to guarantee zero microbial risk.
📋 Allergen Notification: Customers and volunteers are advised to check packaging tags for gluten, dairy, nut, or spice warnings before distribution.
